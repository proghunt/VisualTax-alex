AIIP      Accelerated Investment Incentive Property - For any class (except ZEV classes 54-56) 
          Acquired BEFORE 2025 and after November 20, 2018 
          and became available for use before 2028

DIEP      Designated Immediate Expensing Property: 
          Immediately deduct the full cost of certain assets (up to $}1.5 million).   

HALFYR    Hakf-year rule (or 50%: It limits your CCA deduction in the year you bought the asset. 
          You claim CCA only on half of the Assets the first year.

IMMEXP    Immediate Exoensing: Allows CCPCs to fully write off the cost of some properties in the year they 
          become available for use, up to a $1.5 million.

RIIP      Reaccelerated Investment Incentive Property - Acquired AFTER 2024, and became available for use before 2034.

ZEV       Zero Emmision Vehicles 
          Classes 54 and 55: Acquired AFTER March 18, 2019 and became available for use before 2034.  
          Class 56 applies to a ZEV automotive equipment and vehicles (other than motor vehicles) 
          that you Acquired after March 1, 2020, and that became available for use before 2034.
