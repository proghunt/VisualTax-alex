Multiple Statements/Slips
Steps
VisualTax can open and handle practically an unlimited number of statements such as (T2124, T2032, T776..), slips (T4, T4A...) and other forms that can occur multiple times.

Example: 

Press Alt 4 to open a T4 slip and put some data on it such as Employer Name and Income. You will notice the following: The T4 will appear as T4#1 in the opened forms tab. Also you will notice three new icons. A blue right icon, a blue left arrow and a List icon. They indicate that you are dealing with a multiple occurrence form. The arrows will show the next or previous slip. The List will give you a list of the slips you prepared.

Note: Depending on the Configuration option, you may also see icons for all other slips. This allows you to complete any other slips without having to search for them.

Open another T4 Slip: Press PgDn or Click the blue Right Arrow. It will show you the next slip

List all slips entered: Just click on the 'List' icon. Once the list is shown you can jump directly to any of the slips by clicking any one of them.
