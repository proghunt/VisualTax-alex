Finding a form
Steps

VisualTax offers several ways to find and navigate forms.

Depending on how you work, you can choose one of the following options (or mix them). 

DIRECT WAYS OF FINDING A FORM

  1. Via the Forms Explorer: Press the F2 key or click on the icon provided. This forms explorer shows 'ALL' forms available in one window. Two clicks is the maximum needed to get to any form offered by the program. Most users use this method. The forms window also highlights the forms that have been used already.

  2. Via Form names or Line numbers: Press the F6 key or click on the 'GO' icon

  3. Via the Alphabetic List Icon: Press the F12 key. One click displays the list of the forms in alpha sequence with  English descriptive names. Now either double click on a form or just type some letters such as T4. Used primarily by users who do not remember the form names. For example to find the T4 slip press F12 T4

  4. Via Jump keys: i.e. Alt 4 shows the first T4 slip, Alt 5 shows the first T5 slip   (See the Jump Keys tutorial)

 

INDIRECT WAYS OF FINDING A FORM

  1. Via the Interview mode: You can pre-select the forms you plan to use. Used primarily by preparers who organize/segregate the client documents/slips during the interview process. The interview mode allows you to pre-select the forms you may need based on the user profile such as Pensioner, Student, Employee, etc.

  2. Via the Tax Summary: Press F4 or click the T-Sum icon:  The Tax Summary acts as a menu. Simply double click any item on the Tax Summary. Used primarily by users who like to verify the impact of the entry during preparation.

  3. Via the 'MS Windows Browser style' of the Explorer (in the  Audit Wizard). This shows ALL forms in a MS Windows browser style (expand/collapse)

  4. Via Forms Linking: Green boxes are linked to another form. i.e. if you double click on line 101 the program displays the T4 slips. In addition, you can just press any letter over a linked form and it shows the source form.

  5. Via the Audit Wizard messages: Used to get to a form that has an error. Just click on a message of the Audit Wizard

  6. Via the Previous Forms icon (F7) or the Returns Swapping icon
