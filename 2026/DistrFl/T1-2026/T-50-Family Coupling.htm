Family coupling

Steps:

  1. Open a new or existing return. In the INFO screen set Marital Status to Married or Common-law and complete the spouse's information

  2. Complete the dependants screen (if applicable). Press F2 and Select 'Dependants List'

  3. Click the Family Coupling Set-up icon . This shows the Family set-up screen

  4. In the Family set-up screen:  Enter X next to each dependant (if you want dependants to be part of the coupling process). Press OK.

Note the following:
- A red 'Family ON' button will light up indicating that you are in coupling mode.
- Family members will show up on the Returns list of the left panel.

 Any data you enter could affect all family members.
- The last return of the set-up will show up

- Switching to another return:You can click on the Returns list or click the Swap button . or press the F8 key.
- You can stop the coupling by clicking the red 'Family On' button.

 

Coupling should be used in all cases so the correct Net Income is calculated for both spouses and credits are allocated properly to two spouses. Calculations are automatic. There may be cases where you start with coupling and then you may need to stop coupling and complete each return on its own. Such case comes up when you have situations where due to marital status change, an eligible dependant can be claimed by one of the spouses.
