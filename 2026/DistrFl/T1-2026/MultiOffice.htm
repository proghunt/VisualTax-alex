Visual Tax:  Multi-Office / Multi-user Version

This version of the program benefits tax offices with multiple tax preparers or shift operators, as well as offices controlled by a central location, typical of franchise operations.

The program offers several features to protect the revenue of the tax preparation business of the owner and facilitates control and reporting of results.

 The program also includes additional facilities to identify clients who will benefit from Financial Planning and/or Insurance services. 

The role of the Office Administrator

The program is distributed with a log-in id of ADMIN and password of ADMIN. These should be used for the first time installation of the program on any of the computers. Once the installation is complete, the password ADMIN should be changed.  One of the administrators main functions is to provide passwords for other tax preparers and help them perform functions that otherwise are not allowed by the program. Tax preparers will not be able to perform certain functions unless they know the Administrator's password or the administrator physically enters his password on the preparer's screen when the program prompts for it..

The administrator is empowered with full access to all functions of the program. However, the ADMIN user is not necessarily a tax preparer. In fact, it should be cleaner if no returns are prepared/saved under the ADMIN log-in id.

Multi-office/Multi-user configuration

This item appears under the 'Configuration' item of the main menu. It is accessible only by the administrator or someone who knows the administrator's password. The screen presented allows the administrators to add, change and delete tax preparers for a given installation of the program. Each preparer could be given his/her own Log-in Id, Password, tax data directory and EFile directory. The tax directory could reside on a network drive or on the preparer's hard drives. For better results there should be different tax and Efile directory for each operator although multiple operators can share the same directories.

The EFile RTU numbers are prefixed with the Office code for easy identification of EFile acknowledgements regardless of who received the acknowledgement.

If the owner has multiple offices each office should be identified by a unique four character code. Separate installations within the same office should be identified by a two-character Branch id. Each preparer belongs to an office and Branch.

Example: An owner has two tax offices in Ottawa, and three in Toronto. The following are potential schemes of configuration:

Office: OTTW  Branch: 01, 02

Office: TORO  Branches: 01,02,03

OR

Office: OTT1  Branch: 00

Office: OTT2   Branch: 00

etc.

Remote administrators must be instructed by the Head Office what codes to use for their Office and Branches in their program installations. This coding configuration facilitates consolidation of results at the head office level.

Administrator functions and control features

The administrator's password is needed for the following functions:

- Enter/Modify Preparer's identification data (such as Name, GST number, default charging method, etc.)

- Assign and change login id's and passwords for new preparers

- Delete existing preparers

- Assign or change the starting Invoice Number for each preparer

- Print a return without first saving it

- Save a return on a floppy disk

- Modify the Fees schedule

Consolidation of results

These features are available under the 'Print' item of the main menu.

The system keeps track of the returns saved on a separate directory of the C drive of the tax preparer. Preparers operating from the same computer will have different directories.

This directory is used to drive the consolidation process from all tax preparers in a single office or multiple offices for head office consolidations.

Each operator should submit his daily results to the Office he belongs to at the end of his business day. This submission can be done either by copying the daily results to a network drive of the office or by modem by remote preparers.

Remote offices, should submit their daily results to Head Office at the end of their business day normally by modem.

The receiving office should be running the Visual Tax Modem Communications program in Host mode during these transmissions.
