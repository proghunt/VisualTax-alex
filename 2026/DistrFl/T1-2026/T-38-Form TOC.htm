Form TOC (Table of Contents)
Steps

Some forms contain multiple pages and at times is difficult to locate a specific section that you may need.

 

VisualTax offers the Form Table of Contents (TOC) that helps you locate a specific part of the form.

All multi-page forms and the INFO screen contain a table of contents. When such forms are displayed, a blue 'down pointing arrow' is showing.

 

1. Click on the blue down arrow to display the TOC

2. Double click on the specific section you want to work with
