Introduction - Tutorials

 

First time users of the FP software should start by clicking the 'Help Index'.

 

The objective of the Tutorials is to give you step by step instructions on 'How-To-Do' certain functions of the program. 

It covers basic operations and it is primarily geared to first time users of VisualFP.

 

To start a tutorial:  Click on any of the subjects listed under Tutorials. You can keep the Tutorial opened while running the program. This helps you follow the detail instructions while the return is open. 

Reduce/increase the space the Tutorials occupy: Click on the + or minus icons or drag up or down the horizontal red splitter at the bottom of the tutorial. Also you can leave the tutorial small and use its vertical scroll bar to view its text.

Close the tutorials: Click on the Close button

Re-open the Tutorials any time:  Click Help | Tutorials of the main menu

 

To print a tutorial Right Click on its body and select Print

For additional help click the 'Help Index' button.
