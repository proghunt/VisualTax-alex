Attaching adding tapes 
Steps

You may have a need to keep track of individual items making up a total amount. 

 

You can attach an Adding Tape to a dollar field as follows:

Right click on a dollar field and select 'Attach Adding Tape'.

Enter a reference description, and optionally indicate that the total be posted to the original field.

You can enter any number of item descriptions and dollar amounts. The program will automatically transfer the total amount to the original data field if you indicated so.

 

Finding in what forms/data fields you used adding tapes:

See the Audit Wizard under adding tapes. You can click on any of the items listed.
