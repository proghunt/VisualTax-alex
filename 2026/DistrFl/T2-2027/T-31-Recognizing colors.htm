Recognizing colors
Steps

When you are working with forms you will notice that different fields (cells) may have different colors.

 

VisualTax uses the following conventions:

Black text fields are protected (program calculated). You cannot change its contents 20,000.45
Blue text fields are data you can change   123.78

Green text fields are linked to another form and you cannot change them.

You can go to then other form by pressing the space bar or double clicking the field.

 6,789.78
Yellow background field color indicates erroneous or missing data. They must be corrected. L4W3E
Light yellow background field color indicates a warning. It should be investigated, but it can be ignored.  
Green background field color indicates that the data is supplied by the program via a Pick List such as Province codes, etc. You can open up the pick list my simply entering any letter on the field or double clicking it. Ontario
Red background field color indicates that the data has been flagged for later review, pending consultation with your client or another tax advisor. 345.89
Active field color, as the cursor moves. It can be set to a light pinkish color or light blue (See Configuration | Display)    
The background color of the forms of a return are generally automatic as new returns are loaded. The program is distributed with ten pre-configured colors. You can set a different color for a return (if the color is not already used)  via the Color Grid (upper right-hand corner of the menu).        

 

Overrides: In general VisualTax does not allow you to use blind overrides. CRA is also discouraging it, as it leads to more problems than it solves by breaking the consistency of the data in related forms. However, from a software's vendor point of view it is a convenient way of getting around software bugs... 

 

VisualTax provides 'Explicit Overrides' only in cases where it is conceivable that you may want to deviate from the standard processing offered by the program. You will find such explicit overrides in several places as 'Override (Y/N)'
