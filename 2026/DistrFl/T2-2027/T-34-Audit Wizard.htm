Audit Wizard
Steps

The Audit Wizard monitors the quality of the tax return being prepared. The Audit Wizard by default is always open and alert during tax preparation. Its contents are changing as you enter data or switch from return to return.

 

Closing the Audit Wizard: Press F5. Closing the wizard will provide you with more working space for the normal tax forms being displayed.

Opening the Audit Wizard: Press F5 (Toggle)

The Audit Wizard will be activated automatically during Saving the return or Opening a return. 

Acting on the Wizard diagnostics: Click on the message displayed in the Audit Wizard panel. The program will take you directly on the form and data element that needs your attention.

It is recommended that the Audit Wizard is reviewed before finalizing a tax return.
 The Audit Wizard detects several types of errors and omissions:
Mandatory: Errors in this category must be corrected before you can save the return
Errors: These are significant errors that may cause a filed return to be rejected by CRA
Warnings: These should be checked and determined if applicable. Warnings can be ignored.
Tips: Suggestions that you may want to consider
Review marks: It shows on what forms you entered a review mark
Efile: It shows the reasons/errors a return cannot be Efiled
Adding tapes: It shows the forms/Fields for which you attached an adding tape
Notes: It shows the forms/Fields for which you attached notes
