Multiple Statements/Slips
Steps
There are several forms that can occur multiple times, such as S8 (CCA).

Other multiple forms are S8Free, Notepads, T1163, T1273,  T106SL, Adding tapes, GIFI Balance Sheets, GIFI Income Statements and others.

VisualTax can open and handle practically an unlimited number of such multiple forms.

Example: 

Open the S8 CCA form and enter put some data on it. You will notice the following: The S8 will appear as S8#1 in the opened forms tab. Also you will notice three new icons. A blue right icon, a blue left arrow and a List icon. They indicate that you are dealing with a multiple occurrence form. The arrows will show the next or previous instance of the form. The List will give you a list of the form instances you prepared.

Open another S8 form: Press PgDn or Click the blue Right Arrow. It will show you the next instance of the form.

List all instances entered: Just click on the 'List' icon. Once the list is shown you can jump directly to any of the form instances by clicking any one of them.
