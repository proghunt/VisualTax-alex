Efiling 
Steps

You can Efile a T2 return to CRA.

Note: As of 2006, there is no Internet Filing of Ontario and Alberta returns.

 

Set-up:  Before you can do Internet filing you need to set up the Efile parameters.

Click on Fed-E-File (main menu) and select Set-up Internet Filing. This is a one time step.

Efiling a tax return:

1. Open a tax return and Click on Fed-E-File (main menu) and then click on T183 Corp

2. Set the T183 Efile indicator to Yes (bottom of T183)

3 Click on Fed-E-File and then click on Build and Transmit. Just follow instructions on the screen.
