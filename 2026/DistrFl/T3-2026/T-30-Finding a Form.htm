Finding a form
Steps

VisualTax offers several ways to find and navigate forms.

Depending on how you work, you can choose one of the following options (or mix them). 

DIRECT WAYS OF FINDING A FORM

  1. Via the Forms Explorer: Press the F2 key or click on the icon provided. This forms explorer shows 'ALL' forms available in one window. Two clicks is the maximum needed to get to any form offered by the program. Most users use this method. The forms window also highlights the forms that have been used already.

  2. Via the List on the left side of the screen (in the  Audit Wizard). This shows ALL forms in a MS Windows browser style (expand/collapse)

  3. Via Form names: Press the F6 key or click on the 'GO' icon

 

INDIRECT WAYS OF FINDING A FORM

  1. Via Forms Linking: Green boxes are linked to another form. i.e. if you double click on line 01 of page 2 of the T3 jacket the program displays Schedule 1. In addition, you can just press any letter over a linked form and it shows the source form.

  2. Via the Audit Wizard messages: Used to get to a form that has an error. Just click on a message of the Audit Wizard
