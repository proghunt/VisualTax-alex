NOTICE TO TAXPAYERS
Bill 2, an Act respecting fiscal responsibility which received first reading on November 24, 2003 proposes to:

1) increase the general corporate tax rate to 14 per cent;

2) increase the tax credit for income from manufacturing and processing, mining, logging, farming and fishing (including electrical generation and steam producers) to 2 per cent, thereby increasing the tax rate on this type of income to 12 per cent;

3) maintain the small business tax rate at 5.5%; and

4) increase the business limit to increase the income eligible for the small business rate to 400,000.

All of these proposed changes are effective January 1, 2004. If your corporation has a taxation year that ends before January 1, 2004 your corporation will not be effected by these changes.
